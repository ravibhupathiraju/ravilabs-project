How to read this tab — scores, charts, reversal alerts

Two lenses per ticker: the swing view scores the daily chart (trend structure, momentum, volatility position, volume, breakouts, pullback quality) and the day view scores today's session (VWAP, opening range, first-30-min direction, gap behaviour, intraday RSI, volume pace). Scores run −100…+100; each component's contribution is shown so you can see why. Click a row to expand reasoning and a full trading chart: candles with switchable timeframes (5m / 15m / 4H / 1D), TRAMA(34) adaptive moving average, session VWAP on the intraday timeframes, key levels (day open, prev day high/low, week high/low), auto-drawn trendlines through the last two pivot highs/lows, and an RSI(14) pane with Bollinger bands below — with RSI divergence drawn on both panes when detected. Pattern analyzer (in the chart toolbar) studies where RSI has been bouncing and stalling recently and marks those typical support/resistance RSI zones on the RSI pane. Start reversal alerts watches the entered tickers (every 15 min while the market is open) and fires a desktop toast on reversal evidence — RSI re-crossing 30/70, MACD histogram inflection, price/RSI divergence, reversal candles, VWAP reclaim/loss. Reversal alerts are informational only: they are logged to Alert history but never place paper orders.
